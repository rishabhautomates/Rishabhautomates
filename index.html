Frequently asked questions

Things you may want to know.

What is workflow automation?

It connects tools and actions so repetitive tasks can happen automatically under defined conditions, reducing manual steps.

Which tools can you work with?

My current focus is Google Forms, Google Sheets, Make, and AI tools. Compatibility depends on available integrations and account permissions.

Can you automate my entire business?

Large projects should start with a clear, small workflow. We can assess what is practical and expand gradually.

Will automation always be free?

No. Some platforms have free plans and usage limits. Paid subscriptions, API usage, or other service costs may apply.
